Hardware Engineer

Help us pioneer the next generation of wireless communication using Li-Fi technology.

About the Role

As a Hardware Engineer at Velmenni, you’ll work on cutting-edge Li-Fi communication systems that are reshaping the future of data transmission. You’ll be designing and building hardware solutions that push the boundaries of optical wireless communication.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Design and prototype Li-Fi hardware components including transceivers, modulators, and optical sensors.
  • Collaborate with firmware and software teams to ensure seamless system integration.
  • Conduct testing, validation, and optimization of hardware systems.
  • Develop PCB layouts and work with external manufacturers.
  • Prepare technical documentation and specifications.

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s in Electrical/Electronics Engineering or related field.
  • Strong knowledge of embedded systems, analog/digital circuit design.
  • Experience with optical components and communication systems is a plus.
  • Familiarity with tools like Altium, Eagle, or KiCad.
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.

Location: Gurugram, India

Job Type: Full-Time
