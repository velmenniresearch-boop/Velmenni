Marketing & Communications Manager

Lead our brand's voice and drive impactful marketing campaigns for innovative Li-Fi technology.

About the Role

As a Marketing & Communications Manager at Velmenni, you will craft and execute marketing strategies that elevate our brand and engage customers worldwide. You'll lead communication initiatives and work cross-functionally to align messaging and campaigns.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Develop and implement marketing plans aligned with business goals.
  • Manage brand messaging across digital, print, and events.
  • Lead content creation: blogs, press releases, social media, newsletters.
  • Coordinate with product teams for go-to-market strategies.
  • Analyze campaign effectiveness and optimize ROI.

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Marketing, Communications, or related field.
  • 5+ years experience in marketing or communications, preferably in tech.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Proficiency with digital marketing tools and analytics platforms.
  • Strong project management and organizational abilities.

Location: Gurugram, India

Job Type: Full-Time
